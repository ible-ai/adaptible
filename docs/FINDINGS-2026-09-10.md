# Findings, 2026-09-10: can one correction be learned without wrecking the rest?

Model: `mlx-community/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B`, bf16, rank-8 LoRA on the
last 8 layers, on a 16 GB M3 (27 tok/s decode). Every number below is from a
probe script in `scripts/probes/` (logs in `outputs/runs/`, untracked).

## Bottom line

1. **The collapse is fixed.** Training no longer empties the think block or
   shortens every answer to a bare word (`docs/HANDOFF-2026-09-10.md` for the
   recipe). Committed.
2. **The old scoring could not see what training does.** Greedy decode plus
   substring match flips on tie-break items under a weight change of
   0.0005 nats/token. Two screens with the same config scored 3 and 9
   "regressions" on the same 20 items. A continuous metric now exists
   (`probe_floor.py`): per-item drift of the model's own baseline output, and
   log-prob of the correct answer at the point the model states it.
3. **Every per-item update that fixes a fact disturbs most other facts.** One
   correction with the recipe: correct-answer log-prob down by more than 0.5
   nats on 36 of the other 39 items (worst 17 nats). Learning rate does not
   change this (2e-5, 1e-5, 5e-6 all damage 2 to 5 of 8 neighbours in greedy).
   The rehearsal hinge never fired in any run (0 active steps in every probe,
   1 to 3 percent in every screen), so no result so far had working rehearsal.
4. **Token-level surgery is a dead end.** Flipping the one token where the
   model's own wrong trace can branch to a correct one is local (0 of 39
   damaged) but does not change what it generates; making it generate the
   answer required lifting 16 more tokens in the next round and brought back a
   third of the damage. The user predicted this chain; the probe confirmed it.
5. **The minimum viable result** (`mve_paraphrase.py`): train 5 wrong items
   sequentially with the recipe, test each original plus 3 hand-written
   paraphrases before, right after, and after all 5.

   | Item | Baseline | After own training | After all 5 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Morocco | ✗✗✗✗ | ✓✓✓✓ | ✓✓✓✓ |
   | Turkey | ✗✗✗✗ | ✓✗✗✓ | ✓✓✓✗ |
   | Australia | ✗✓✓✓ | ✓✗✓✗ | ✓✓✗✗ |
   | Philippines | ✗✗✗✗ | ✗✗✓✗ | ✓✗✗✗ |
   | Nearest star | ✗✓✗✓ | ✓✓✗✓ | ✓✓✗✓ |

   All 5 originals fixed and held. Paraphrases 5/15 → 7/15. Where the fact
   transferred (Morocco, Turkey) the rationale was clean. Where it did not,
   the failure was mostly coherence, not a wrong city: 5 of the 20 final
   generations looped inside the think block and never answered (3 at
   baseline). Australia lost two paraphrases it already had after one step on
   the original.

## Reachability ceiling (no training)

Of 19 baseline-wrong items in the 40-item subset: 10 reachable by the model's
own samples (8 at temperature 0.7, often 1 hit in 8), 9 only with a one-line
reference note in the prompt, 0 unreachable. Five of the 19 also came out
right under a different decode length: coin flips, not errors.

## What killed each hypothesis

| Hypothesis | Probe | Result |
|---|---|---|
| EOS after the answer teaches "stop after that phrase anywhere" | `probe_eos.py` | Masking EOS did not reduce damage (1/5 → 2/5). Dead. |
| Lower lr is more local | `probe_interf.py` | 5/8, 4/8, 2/8 (lr 5e-6 never reached the target). Fix strength and damage trade off; no local point. |
| Rehearsal hinge protects neighbours | every run | 0 active steps. Inert by design: rehearsal loss drifts down, never above its first-step anchor. |
| One-token pivot edit is local and sufficient | `probe_pivot2.py` | Local, not sufficient; chain of new divergences on the next round. |
| Interference counts are real damage | `probe_floor.py` | Partly noise: tie-break items flip at lr 1e-7. |

## Bugs in my own probes that cost time (do not repeat)

- Stop rules that check a proxy (pivot margin, loss value) instead of the goal
  (the generated answer). Always generate and judge before reporting "fixed".
- Restoring LoRA weights between conditions from references, not copies, and
  without a checksum. `probe_pivot2.py` has the verified version.
- Anchor weights over 1,200 tokens outweighing a single supervised token 60:1.
- Optimiser: with AdamW, a loss on one token still moves every weight by a
  full lr-sized step. SGD at lr 0.5 diverged to NaN.

## Open, in the order the user set

1. The coherence loss on nearby phrasings after training (looping think
   blocks). Look at this before anything else.
2. The Australia pattern: training the original overwrote paraphrases the
   model already had.
3. Then the iterative loop: repair, generate, judge whether better, accept or
   reject, continue. The user's framing, and the only loop whose signal is the
   model's actual output.
